# p2 Motivation

The first ChineseBabyLM Challenge aims training data-efficient and cognitively plausible language models for Chinese language. Our shared task is inspired by BabyLM challenge, which studies sample-efficient pretraining under data budgets inspired by human language acquisition. BabyLM challenge focus mainly on English language. We extend their idea, and expand it to Chinese language. 


# p3 Evaluation Tracks

ChineseBabyLM evaluates models along three complementary tracks, with 14 tasks in total. First, the NLU track measures Chinese syntactic and semantic understanding. 

The Natural Language Understanding track combines zero-shot minimal-pair evaluation, including ZhoBLiMP, with supervised Chinese understanding tasks from CLUE.f

The Hanzi track directly tests whether models acquire structural and phonological knowledge about Chinese characters from limited input. 

The Cognitive Modeling track uses Chinese fMRI benchmarks from MulCogBench to measure how well model representations predict human neural responses during word- and discourse-level language comprehension. 

These tracks are intended to reward models that are not only accurate on downstream tasks, but also sensitive to Chinese-specific linguistic structure and aligned with cognitively motivated evaluation criteria.
